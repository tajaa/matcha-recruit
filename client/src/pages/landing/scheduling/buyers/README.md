# Scheduling landing page

`/` is the scheduling home page, with setup/integrations, staffing recovery examples, operational fit, a three-input value calculator, proposed pricing, and practical FAQs alongside the scheduling demonstrations. `/scheduling-v2` redirects to `/`, preserving query parameters and section links. The home page uses the canonical root URL and is indexable; `/incidents` retains its existing content.

The $49 Basic / $99 Pro / $149 Autopilot prices and inclusions are proposals. This change does not create subscriptions, grant product features, or change billing. Plan CTAs open the existing consultation form. The current self-serve signup remains the same destination.

Square finalized sales and CSV imports are described precisely. No native Toast, time clock, actual-break verification, payroll export, or automatically bundled HR/incident capabilities are promised. First-account roster import and later pay-rate import are distinguished.

The value calculator asks for three numbers: locations, the average hourly wage of the managers who build schedules, and the hours spent building the schedule per location each week. It always prices Autopilot, the highest plan, and says so. The price comes from the same plan definitions as the pricing section.

It assumes no saving. It shows what that manager time costs today (weekly hours converted to months with 52 / 12, scaled by locations), the Autopilot subscription, and the minutes a week per location that would have to come back to cover the subscription. When that break-even exceeds the time entered, it says the plan would not pay for itself on schedule-building time alone. Invalid or incomplete inputs suppress the estimate. The starting figures are editable placeholders, not customer results. Volume discounts and taxes are excluded. No customer results, logos, testimonials, or screenshots are invented.

Before removing the pricing-preview labels or publishing customer results, confirm actual product inclusions, final pricing and employee limits, integration setup/service scope, supported jurisdiction coverage, and a measured customer example with permission to publish.

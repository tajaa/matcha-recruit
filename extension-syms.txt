3. Minor work permits.
- Problem: a minor without a valid permit is hard-blocked from scheduling. Today the manager ticks "on file", and Matcha stores no copy.
- Recipient: usually a parent, who will never have a login, so this is where sym-link fits best.
- Friction: the company passcode is shared on the wall or in a channel, and a parent sees neither. The manager would have to relay it by phone or through the minor.
- With email: the permit (the minor's date of birth, school) sits in an inbox, and Matcha still has no record of it.
- Cost: needs a database migration, which you'd approve before it runs.
